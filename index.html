Advanced and privacy
This URL is not on this computer: the sentence text and candidate words would be sent to another host.

It only chooses between words the camera itself considered, and never changes a word the camera was sure of. Filled words are shown in [brackets?]. Video never leaves your device. The in-browser LLM downloads its model files once from huggingface.co (about 945 MB of GPU memory when running; WebGPU required); no speech, video or text of yours is sent anywhere. The Ollama engine sends the sentence with one gap, the candidate words and up to two earlier sentences to the Ollama URL above (default: this computer), never video or mouth data.

      Known limits

      • Lips alone are ambiguous. Many sounds look identical (p/b/m, t/d/n). This is why the vocabulary is small and fixed.
      • Speaker-dependent. It only works after you calibrate it on your own lips; another person will get poor results.
      • Lighting and angle matter. Use even light and a centered, front-facing camera.
      • Never guesses. A low-confidence or unfamiliar movement shows "?" and says nothing.
      • Private. Video stays in your browser; calibration is stored locally and can be deleted.
